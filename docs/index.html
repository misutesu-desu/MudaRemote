⚡ MudaRemote: Mudae Automation for Discord

A desktop tool for automating rolls, claims, Kakera collection, and multi-account presets.

Disclaimer & Safety Notice

This is a self-bot tool. Self-bots violate Discord's Terms of Service. Using this software carries ban risk. Provided for educational purposes only.

🚀 Core Capabilities

MudaRemote handles repetitive Mudae bot mechanics with sophisticated regex matching, priority crystal collection, and cross-account concurrency control.

🎯 Wishlist & Series Sniping
Automated matching on wished characters, favorite series, or value thresholds ($500+). Includes instant self-claim and panic claiming before reset.
💎 Kakera Power Management
Clicks crystals according to customizable priority hierarchy (Light > Yellow > Orange > Red > Rainbow) while strictly preserving minimum power.
🔮 Heuristic Sphere Solver
Solves $oh (Sphere Harvest) and $oc (Red Sphere Clue Board) mini-games automatically using constraint elimination algorithms.
👥 Multi-Account Concurrency
ClaimCoordinator synchronizes claim reservations across multiple tokens/presets, preventing double snipes and shared reset desyncs.

Deployment & Mobile Setup Guide

🖥️ Windows Standalone Setup

  1. Download MudaRemote.exe from the Releases tab.
  2. Double-click to launch the Preset Editor.
  3. Enter your Channel ID, select your roll command ($wa, /wa), and customize your Kakera thresholds.
  4. Click Save & Start Bot.

🐍 Python / Source Setup (Cross-Platform)

git clone https://github.com/misutesu-desu/MudaRemote.git
cd MudaRemote
pip install -r requirements.txt

To run the GUI editor:

python mudae_preset_editor.py

To run headlessly / CLI (supports single or all presets):

python mudae_bot.py --all

📱 Android Setup (APK or Termux)

Method A: Native APK (Recommended)

  1. Download and install app-debug.apk from Releases.
  2. Configure profiles and start the foreground service for 24/7 background execution.

Method B: Termux Mobile Setup (Community Guide)

Support Desk Knowledge Base & Solutions

❓ Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

Compiled directly from real support tickets, community solutions, and the developer's guide.

Why is the bot claiming random characters not on my wishlist or below minimum Kakera? ▼
This is almost always caused by one of three settings:
  • Panic Claim (snipe_ignore_min_kakera_reset): When enabled, if your claim resets within 60 minutes, the bot will claim the highest-value available character regardless of min_kakera to avoid wasting a claim right ("use it or lose it"). Disable this toggle if you strictly want only wishlist/high-value claims.
  • Own Rolls vs Other Players: min_kakera applies to your own rolls. If you want a threshold for stealing from other players, configure Value Sniping Threshold (kakera_snipe_threshold).
  • Rank Limits: Check if max_claim_rank or max_like_rank is enabled in your claim rules.
Why is the bot skipping Kakera crystals even though I have 100% power? ▼
Check the following common causes:
  • Chaos Mode (only_chaos): When enabled, the bot only clicks crystals on characters with 10+ keys (chaos keys with 50% power discount). It deliberately ignores normal rolls.
  • Custom Kakera Emojis list: Check Kakera Emojis under Advanced Settings. If a color (e.g. kakeraP, kakeraY, kakeraO) was deleted from the list, the bot will ignore it.
  • Power Thresholds (kakera_power_thresholds): If you set a minimum power requirement for a specific color (e.g. Yellow: 200%), the bot will skip it when power is below that number.
  • $mk Only Mode (mk_only): If turned on, the bot restricts clicks exclusively to crystals that drop from $mk rolls.
How does Smart Timing (time_rolls_to_claim_reset) work? ▼
If your claim right is already available, the bot rolls immediately. If your claim is on cooldown but will reset within 60 minutes, the bot calculates your total roll duration and starts rolling at the exact minute needed so that your last roll finishes right after your claim resets. This ensures you can claim any high-value card rolled with your fresh claim right.
How do I prevent the bot from sending $limroul on startup? ▼
In the Preset Editor under Power & Expert / Misc, turn on Fast Start (skip_initial_commands: true). This skips sending $limroul, $dk, and startup calibration commands on bot launch.
How do I configure multiple accounts (alts) without collisions? ▼
Create a separate preset for each account with its own Discord Token and Channel ID. Set a staggered Wait Before Starting (start_delay, e.g. Account 1: 0s, Account 2: 15s, Account 3: 30s). The built-in ClaimCoordinator lock coordinates claim reservations across all accounts so they never double-claim or race for the same character.
Why does the bot send $tu and can it be stopped? ▼
$tu is mandatory on initial startup and after hourly resets because it provides the authoritative state: claim timer, roll reset, available rolls, $rt cooldown, Kakera power %, and $dk stock. In version 4.6+, the bot uses an intelligent status freshness ledger and will never repeat $tu unnecessarily during normal rolling. You can also specify a Command Channel ID (command_channel_id) to keep your main rolling channel clean.
How does Smart Power Refill ($dk) work? ▼
To use Smart Refill, enable both Auto $dk (auto_dk_enabled) and Smart Power Refill (dk_power_management). Instead of spamming $dk the second it is off cooldown, the bot checks your current power and click costs. When your power drops below the threshold needed for a crystal click, it automatically sends $dk to restore power (requires Gold IV badge in Mudae).
What is the difference between snipe_mode and reactive_snipe_on_own_rolls? ▼
  • snipe_mode: Monitors messages from other users in configured channels and steals matching wishlist or high-value characters they roll.
  • reactive_snipe_on_own_rolls: Monitors your own rolls in real time. When a wishlist hit appears, it clicks the claim button instantly without waiting for your entire roll batch to complete.
How does the Heuristic Sphere Game Solver work ($oh & $oc)? ▼
For $oh (Sphere Harvest), MudaRemote prioritizes free purple clicks and valuable revealed spheres according to your preference order. For $oc (Red Sphere Clue Board), it analyzes revealed clue numbers and eliminates candidate tiles to pinpoint the red sphere safely within 5 clicks.
How does the Kakera Forcedivorce Farm Loop work? ▼
If you have server admin/mod permissions, the bot forcedivorces your chosen high-value character (Emerald IV gives you 100% of their kakera value upon claiming them again while keeping all their keys). The bot can release the farm character before starting a roll cycle or immediately after a verified claim.
Error: BotBase.__init__() missing 1 required keyword-only argument: 'intents' ▼
This error occurs if standard discord.py is installed instead of the self-bot fork. Fix it by running:
pip uninstall discord.py
pip install discord.py-self
Error: 404 Not Found (error code: 10003): Unknown Channel ▼
The Discord Channel ID or Command Channel ID entered in your preset editor does not exist, or the Discord account token you provided does not have permissions to read/send messages in that channel. Double-check your channel IDs (Right click channel in Discord → Copy Channel ID with Developer Mode on).
Does my PC have to stay on, and can I turn off my monitor? ▼
Yes, your PC must remain powered on with sleep/hibernate disabled in Windows power settings. You can safely turn off your physical monitor or run headlessly on a VPS / Android device (via the APK or Termux).
Why should I use Slash Commands (/wa) and what roll speed should I use? ▼
Mudae awards extra Kakera bonuses and increased wish spawn rates when using slash commands (/wa, /ha, /mk). When using slash commands, set Rolling Speed to at least 2.0 seconds in the editor to avoid Discord interaction rate limits.
