**🆕 MudaRemote v4.9.1-beta.10**

### 🐛 Bug Fixes
- **Delayed wish detection:** Snipe mode now checks wishes added through message edits, so a late “Wished by” ping can trigger a claim while the status timer sleeps.
- **Manual $rt recovery:** Eligible characters arriving while your manual $rt is being confirmed are kept for a claim retry, even when automatic $rt use is restricted.
### ⚡ Improvements
- **Clearer claim logs:** See when an edited wish is detected and when Mudae confirms your manual $rt, making claim activity easier to follow.
