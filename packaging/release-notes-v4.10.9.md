**🆕 MudaRemote v4.10.9**

### ✨ New Features
- **Auto `$oq`:** The bot can now play your `$oq` boards. It works out where the purple spheres can be from the clues and goes for the 3 purples that turn the last one red.
- **Auto `$ot`:** The bot can now play your `$ot` boards. Only blue costs a click there, so it follows the color lines and avoids blue as much as it can.

Turn them on under Power & Expert Settings. Both are off by default.

### 🐛 Bug Fixes
- **No More Broken APK Download:** Fetch app updates could say a new APK was available and send you to a page that didn't exist. The bot code still updates on its own like before, and the app now only mentions an APK that's really there.
- **APK Downloads From Switch Version:** With the next Android app, releases that come with an APK show "+ APK" in Switch Version, where you can choose to update the bot code or download the APK.

Restart the bot after updating.
