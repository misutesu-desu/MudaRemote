**🆕 MudaRemote v4.10.9-beta.1**

### ✨ New Features
- **Auto `$oq`:** The bot can now play your `$oq` boards. It works out where the purple spheres can be from the clues and goes for the 3 purples that turn the last one red.
- **Auto `$ot`:** The bot can now play your `$ot` boards. Only blue costs a click there, so it follows the color lines and avoids blue as much as it can.

Turn them on under Power & Expert Settings. Both are off by default.

Restart the bot after updating.
