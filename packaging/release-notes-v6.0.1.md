**MudaFork v6.0.1**

### Fixed
- **Alts are your extra account tokens.** The first token of a profile is the main; the tokens under «Account Tokens (multi-account)» are its alts. Giving kakera, spheres and harem pages, side rolls and fast setup are set once in the profile and run on the alts. Nothing is greyed out any more.
- **The main no longer seems to stop for no reason.** When «Pause on Key Limit» pauses an account, the log now names the account, the reason and when it resumes, and turning the switch off ends the pause at once.
- **Screen repairs:** header buttons are no longer cut, the engine line shows its version, the bottom bar no longer covers content, and each setting shows its description under its title.

### New
- **Main claims good rolls made by its alts**, and **alts claim good rolls the main leaves** — two options, both off by default. Only one account ever clicks.
- **Let side accounts upgrade too:** alts upgrade perk 10 only with this on, and then keep their spheres.
- **Key limit pauses:** «everything» (as before) or «rolls only».
- Logs of a multi-account profile start each line with «main:» or «alt N:».

### Lighter
- The app is built for phones only (arm64), which makes the download smaller.
