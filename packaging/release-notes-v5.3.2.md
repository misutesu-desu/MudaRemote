**🆕 MudaRemote v5.3.2**

### ✨ New Features
- **Auto `$p` Setup Warning:** If Auto `$p` is on but your `$tu` doesn't show the `$p` line, the bot now tells you to fix it with `$tuarrange`. Before, Auto `$p` just never fired and you had no idea why.

### 🐛 Bug Fixes
- **Bot Keeps Going After A Reconnect:** When Discord dropped and resumed the connection, the bot could sit idle until you restarted it. It now picks up rolling and sniping again right after the connection comes back.

Restart the bot after updating.
