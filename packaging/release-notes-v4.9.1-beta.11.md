**🆕 MudaRemote v4.9.1-beta.11**

### 🐛 Bug Fixes
- **Auto $rt on shorter claim timers:** Auto $rt After Claim now works with a 60-minute claim cycle, including the reported case with 40 minutes left until reset. Keep claiming without needing to restore your claim manually.
### ⚡ Improvements
- **Smarter $rt saving:** The near-reset saving window now adjusts to your configured claim interval: 20 minutes for an hourly cycle. The usual three-hour cycle keeps its existing 60-minute saving window.
