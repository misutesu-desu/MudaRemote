**🆕 MudaFork v6.0.0**

First release of MudaFork, a fork of MudaRemote (MIT).

### 🆕 What the fork adds
- **Side rolls and gives:** extra roll and give handling on top of the original bot.
- **Perk 10 upgrade:** automatic perk-10 upgrade (games first, final stock, minimum 2000).
- **Fast setup:** a shorter first-run setup.
- **Logs:** one log line shape with kinds and redaction, daily TXT files, filters and export.
- **Keep running:** the Android app keeps the bot running on phones that kill background apps.

### 🔁 Updates
- Updates now come only from the MudaFork releases on GitHub. The Android app installs next to MudaRemote under its own app id (`com.mudafork.android`).

Restart the bot after updating.
