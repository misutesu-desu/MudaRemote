**🆕 MudaRemote v4.9.1-beta.14**

### ✨ New Features
- **Shop 7 Double Rewards Only:** A new optional checkbox collects blue-background Chaos Kakera buttons that give double rewards from Shop perk 7. Available on Windows and Android, for immediate clicks, queued collection, and eligible snipes.
- **Combine your filters:** Keep your chosen Kakera colors and power limits. Enable the existing 50% discount filter separately if you also want to require discounted reactions. Purple Kakera and spheres keep their existing collection settings.
