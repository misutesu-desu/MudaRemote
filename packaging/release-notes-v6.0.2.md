**MudaFork v6.0.2**

### Fixed
- **Perk 10 no longer sends a command without a character.** One character is upgraded with `$oi Name $ 10`, several with `$oic`. A command is never sent unless every name is real, and «No character found!» is treated as a refusal.
- **Setting titles are no longer cut** when they wrap to a second line.
- **Gives and upgrades are confirmed correctly** when the bot ticks the command instead of the answer.
- «Give all kakera» is no longer a button on the Profiles card.

### New
- **Main keeps rolling while an alt can claim**: an alt with a claim or `$rt` takes the main's good rolls.
- **Main and alts take each other's kakera.**
- **Key farming:** the main and its alts roll independently.
- **Perk 10:** choose how many characters to upgrade each time, and «Upgrade perk 10 now».
- **Kakera give:** choose «give everything» or «give only the amount».
- **The `$mmz-` page buttons** are recognised in more ways, and the log says what the list showed when a page cannot be turned.

### App
- **Updates download faster and resume** after a dropped connection, with the speed shown.
- The restart notice now reads «Android closed MudaFork at … It started again at …», with the reason when Android gives one.

All new options are off until you turn them on.
