**🆕 MudaRemote v5.1.4**

### ✨ New Features
- **Ouroperk 5 Emoji Selection:** New Custom Ouroperk 5 Emoji Override in the emoji settings. Pick which kakera the bot clicks on Ouroperk 5 characters. Leave it unchecked and nothing changes.

### 🐛 Bug Fixes
- **No More Random Claims After Panic Roll:** With Lurker Strategy, the "claim anything" panic mode before your claim reset could stay on into the next claim round, so the bot could grab a low value character, even one you rolled yourself. Your Min Kakera now comes back as soon as the claim resets.

Restart the bot after updating.
