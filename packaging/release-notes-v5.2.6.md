**🆕 MudaRemote v5.2.6**

### ✨ New Features
- **Turn Off Event Cards:** Auto-Claim Free Claims and Event Cards now also stops the bot clicking free cards like the Halloween candy. Handy for snipe-only accounts.
- **`$arlp` On A Schedule:** Send $arlp After Every N $kl releases your pins after a set number of `$kl`, so you don't lose a command to the pin error. The bot still sends `$arlp` if the error shows up earlier.

### 🐛 Bug Fixes
- **Auto `$dk` Time Stays Saved:** The time no longer disappears from the field when you reopen a preset, so saving again won't erase it.
- **Panic Rolls Start On Time:** The Timing Variation wait can no longer use up the Lurker panic window and push your rolls past the reset.
- **Fewer Wasted `$us` Rolls:** Panic now starts early enough to fit your saved rolls, and the bot only pulls what it can finish before the roll reset.
- **Sphere Games In Later Rounds:** New `$oh`, `$oc`, `$oq` and `$ot` stock gets played without restarting the bot.

Restart the bot after updating.
