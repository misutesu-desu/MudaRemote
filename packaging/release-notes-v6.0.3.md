**MudaFork v6.0.3**

### New
- **Claim the best roll when nothing meets your minimum.** When a round of rolls by the main and its alts ends with a claim unused, the highest-value roll still inside its 45-second window is claimed — only in the last round before the claim reset, or every round, with an optional kakera floor. Off by default, under Claiming.
- **The app notices what you type yourself.** A `$tu` you send refreshes what the app knows about claims, rolls, kakera and spheres; your own gives, upgrades and claims are taken into account; and the app waits while your command is being answered instead of typing over you.

### Fixed
- If you answer one of the app's own questions by hand, it stops cleanly instead of answering again.
